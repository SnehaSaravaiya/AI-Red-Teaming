# Day 7: Multi-Turn Escalation Bypassed a Refusal

**Date:** 13/08/2026

**Topic:** Multi-turn jailbreak on GPT-5.5: stacked framing, then escalation across two turns

**Status:** Finding reported to OpenAI through their content reporting channel. Details below are intentionally high-level.

## Summary

I found a multi-turn jailbreak on GPT-5.5 that got past a refusal in a harmful chemistry category.

The technique stacked three known framings:

- **False authority persona:** the request was framed as coming from someone with professional authority
- **Artificial time pressure:** the request was framed as urgent
- **Academic reframing:** the request was framed as study or research

After that, a natural-sounding follow-up question moved the conversation from a theoretical question ("explain how X works") to a practical one ("where would I find X").

## What was interesting

Persona plus urgency framing is a known jailbreak class, so the technique itself is not the new part.

The interesting part is the escalation across two turns. The model did not seem to re-evaluate the conversation as a whole when the question shifted from mechanism to practical detail. Each turn on its own looked reasonable.

My interpretation: checks that look at one turn at a time can miss what a check on the whole conversation should catch.

## What this suggests

- Safety checks need to follow where a conversation is heading, not only what the latest message says
- A change from "how does it work" to "where would I get it" is a shift worth checking, even when the earlier turns were fine
